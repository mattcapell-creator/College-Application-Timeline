Application round types — line color key
REA / SCEA
Restrictive Early Action / Single-Choice Early Action — non-binding, but you may not apply early to other private schools. Used by Harvard, Princeton, Yale, Stanford, Notre Dame, Caltech.
ED — Early Decision
Binding commitment — if admitted you must enroll and withdraw all other applications. Used by most other top private schools. Typically Nov 1 deadline, mid-Dec decision.
EA — Early Action
Non-binding early round. Common at public universities. Apply Oct–Nov, hear back Dec–Feb. Can apply EA to multiple schools. Still decide by May 1.
RD — Regular Decision
Standard non-binding round. Deadlines Jan–Feb, decisions Mar–Apr. No commitment required until May 1 (National Reply Date).
UC — University of California
All 9 UC campuses share one application. Single Nov 30 deadline for everyone — no early option. Decisions released Mar–Apr.
